What makes a deal actually work

The vendors who do well here follow the same pattern:

Give one thing free with a purchase — don't discount everything. "Free drink with any plate" costs you about sixty cents and feels like a gift. "15% off" costs you more and feels like a coupon.

Be specific. "Free birria taco with any plate" beats "great Mexican food."

Say where you are. "The red truck by Gate 3." People are looking for you in a crowd.
