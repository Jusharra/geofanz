The short version

We check your location to see if you're at the venue. We keep a rough, non-identifying record that a visit happened. We don't know who you are, we don't sell anything about you, and we don't track you after you leave.

What we collect

Your location, when you allow it. Your browser sends us your coordinates so we can check whether you're inside the event zone. We use the precise reading for that check, then store only a rounded version — accurate to roughly the neighborhood, not the address.

Anonymous visit information. A temporary ID for your visit, which deals you viewed or unlocked, your device type, and the time. The visit ID isn't connected to your name, phone, or email.

Redemption records. If you unlock a deal, we create a one-time code. When a vendor scans it, we record that it was used, when, and where.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, address, phone, or email — unless you type it into a form
  • Any payment information
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-site tracking cookies
  • Your location at any time other than when you have this site open

We do not track you in the background. We cannot see where you go after you close this page.

Why we collect it

To decide whether to show you deals, to prevent one code being used many times, and to tell vendors how their offer performed — as totals only. A vendor never receives information about an individual person.

Who we share it with

Nobody, except the services that run the site: Supabase for the database and Netlify for hosting. We do not sell personal information, and we never have.

How long we keep it

Visit records: 12 months. Redemption records: 24 months. Anything you submit through a form: until you ask us to delete it.

Your rights in California

California residents may request to know what we hold about you, request deletion, or request correction. Because we don't collect names, we may not be able to link a request to specific records — but we'll do what we can. Email [PRIVACY EMAIL].

Turning location off

Deny the permission prompt, or change it in your browser settings for this site. The site will still load — you just won't see deals.

Children

This site isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their information.

We'll update the date at the top when this changes.
