How It Works

1. Be at the game.
Hot Hand Buys only works inside the venue and the surrounding lots. That's the point — these deals aren't posted anywhere else.

2. Allow location.
We check where you are to confirm you're at the venue. That's it. We don't track you, we don't save your address, and we don't follow you after you leave.

3. Tap a deal to unlock it.
You'll get a code and a QR square on your screen.

4. Show it to the vendor.
They scan it, you get your deal. Each code works once.

Deals end when the event ends. No extensions — that's what makes it a Hot Hand.

Not seeing anything?
You may be outside the zone, or nothing's running right now. Try again closer to the venue.

Said no to location by mistake?
Reload the page and tap Allow, or turn location back on for this site in your browser settings.
